# AnvilEvent

When performing a single operation at a landing position, distinguish an entire giant anvil from individual hit blocks. Normal `OnLand` provides the anvil's landing position, with the pressed block at `getPos().below()`. After a giant anvil lands, `GiantOnLand` fires once, followed by nine `OnLand` events across the 3 × 3 base; subscribe to the former when settling once for the whole structure. The anvil hammer also has a path that posts `OnLand` using a temporary entity.

The poster for normal falling anvils reads the flag corresponding to `OnLand.setAnvilDamage(true)` and updates the landing spot via the anvil damage flow; the giant anvil's nine notifications do not perform this read-back. `CollisionBlock` reports a collision after movement finishes, where `speed` is the length of the movement vector before collision. Canceling it blocks subsequent built-in collision crafting listeners, though already-executed entity movement cannot be undone by it. `HurtEntity` provides the target and damage value as read-only fields.
